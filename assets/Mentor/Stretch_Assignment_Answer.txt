Why would you be interested in this stretch assignment?

I love missionary work. I have seen the Savior change lives, including my own, and I want to spend my time helping missionaries feel that same power in their teaching.

As a mentor, I've learned that missionaries grow most when they know someone truly cares about them. Two missionaries once opened up to me about their worries before an upcoming baptism. Because we had built trust, they went forward with faith and the help of the ward members. One group committed to follow up with seven people every day and brought seven friends to church meetings when their goal was five. A sister missionary learned to ask inspired questions and came back saying her lessons had changed.

As Assistant to the Mission President, I served under two mission presidents and through the transition between them. I learned to minister to leaders one by one, help them with their problems, and bring out the best in them. That is the leadership I see in Jesus Christ, and it is what I love most.

That is why I want to be a Mentor Trainer. I want to help other mentors become the best version of themselves and learn to teach by the Spirit, so their missionaries feel it too. I want to help them align every session with the vision statement and see tangible results. I also want them to understand why the participation indicator matters and to follow up consistently, so that missionaries build a real relationship of trust with their mentor. When mentors stay centered on Jesus Christ, more missionaries go to the field prepared to invite others to come unto Him.
